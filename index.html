Welcome to Code-A-Thone 2025

Date: June 15-17, 2025

About Schedule Speaker Contact

Hackathon 2025 is a tech innovation event where students, developers, and creators come together to build smart solutions for the future of Electric Vehicles and sustainable technology. Our goal is to promote creativity, problem-solving, and hands-on learning through real-world challenges. Participants collaborate, code, innovate, and present impactful ideas that can shape tomorrow’s mobility.

Keynote Speaker

  • Dr. Alex Johnson, a leading expert in Electric Vehicle technology and sustainable innovation, will be our keynote speaker. With over 20 years of experience in the automotive industry, Dr. Johnson has been at the forefront of EV research and development, contributing to several groundbreaking projects that have shaped the future of transportation.

  • Dr. Johnson will share insights on the latest trends in EV technology, the challenges and opportunities in the field, and the role of innovation in driving sustainable mobility solutions. Attendees will have the chance to learn from his vast experience and gain inspiration for their own projects during the hackathon.

  • Join us to hear Dr. Johnson's vision for the future of electric vehicles and how technology can pave the way for a greener, more sustainable world.
